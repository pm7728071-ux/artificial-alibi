# ARTIFICIAL ALIBI — launch kit

## One-liner
A psychological interrogation game where the lie detector is his own heart, and the
suspect you're sure about may not be the one who did it.

## Store blurb (short)
Station Argus-9, 02:14. The main generator is scrap. One engineer says he was fixing pipes
in Sector B. Three exhibits say otherwise. Ask the right questions, watch his pulse, and
decide whether to lock him up — before the batteries fail.

## Store blurb (long)
ARTIFICIAL ALIBI is a single-session interrogation game built around one uncomfortable
idea: you can never be certain. Three suspects sit in the same freezing room. A lead
engineer with a cut coolant seal. A biologist with a pathogen loose in Lab 3. A synthetic
medic with records that don't match the bodies.

Each case gives you a live surveillance feed, a heart-rate monitor, three pieces of
physical evidence and a transcript. Press the wrong nerve and his pulse spikes. Catch him
in a contradiction and something in his face changes. You get one verdict: **LOCK HIM UP**
or **RELEASE SUSPECT**.

In RANDOMIZED MYSTERY mode the guilt itself is hidden. Every exhibit still lands. Every
contradiction still registers. You may be building an airtight case against an innocent
person, and the mission report will say so.

## Features
- Three hand-written cases, each with its own exhibits, dialogue bank and truth
- Live BPM monitor, stress states, and a contradiction system that caps at three
- Animated surveillance avatar: breathing, blinking, sweat, jitter, red interference
- Full voice acting through your browser's speech synthesis, cast per suspect
- Play with your microphone: speak your questions, the game transcribes and asks them
- Randomized Mystery Mode with four distinct verdict outcomes
- Settings, save and continue, pause menu, and shareable deep links per case

## Controls
- Type a question, press Enter or INTERROGATE
- 🎙️ SPEAK: interrogate by voice (Chrome/Edge, needs mic permission)
- Click an exhibit to confront
- Esc: pause · SOUND FX: mute voice and audio

## Requirements
Any modern browser. Voice needs speech synthesis (all major browsers); microphone input
needs Chrome or Edge over HTTPS. No install, no account, no server — it runs entirely in
the page and works offline once loaded.

## Known limitations
- Voice quality depends on the system voices installed; Chrome on desktop gives the best
  results, Linux/some mobile defaults sound flatter.
- Speech recognition is not available in Firefox or Safari; the button disables itself.
- No mid-case saving: a case is one sitting, by design.
